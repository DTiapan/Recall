# Q2 2026 Quarterly Financial Report — Recall Inc.

**Filing:** 10-Q (unaudited)  
**Period End:** 2026-06-30

## Executive Summary

Recall Inc. delivered strong top-line growth in Q2 2026 driven by enterprise RAG platform adoption. Total revenue reached **$42.7M**, up 38% year-over-year.

## Key Metrics

| Metric | Q2 2026 | Q2 2025 | Change |
|--------|---------|---------|--------|
| Total Revenue | $42.7M | $30.9M | +38% |
| **EBITDA** | $8.1M | $4.2M | +93% |
| **EBITDA margin** | 19.0% | 13.6% | +5.4 pp |
| Gross Margin | 78% | 74% | +4 pp |
| Operating Cash Flow | $6.4M | $2.8M | +129% |

## Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)

**ARR** grew to **$156M**, with the **enterprise segment** contributing 62% of net-new ARR. Average contract value (ACV) for deals over $250K increased to $410K.

## Guidance

Management reiterates FY2026 revenue guidance of $175–$180M and expects EBITDA margin expansion to 21–22% by Q4 2026 as inference costs decline.

## Risk Factors

Macroeconomic uncertainty and lengthening enterprise sales cycles remain primary risks. Foreign exchange headwinds reduced reported revenue by approximately $0.9M in Q2.
